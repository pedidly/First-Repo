# Listing copy

Paste-ready. Swap the bracketed bits. Every listing leads with what the buyer
gets, not with the printer — nobody outside the hobby cares what an A1 is.

Photograph on a plain surface in daylight, near a window, never under a ceiling
light. Three shots per listing: the thing, the thing in use, the thing next to a
hand or a coffee cup for scale.

## 7. The service listing — the one that earns most

**How it works**

1. Send a photo of the broken part next to a ruler, and say what it belongs to.
2. I tell you within a day whether I can make it, and what it costs.
3. You have it inside a week.

Most repairs land between €30 and €60, against a few hundred for a new

**Tags:** 3d printing, repair, spare parts, appliance parts, custom parts, prototyping

## Photo shot list

| Shot | Why |
|---|---|
| Straight down, plain background, daylight | The listing thumbnail. This one sells it. |
| In use — on the door, on the collar, on the tree | Shows scale and context |
| Next to a hand or a coffee cup | Answers "how big is it" without a caption |
| A close-up of the raised lettering at an angle | Proves it is not a printed sticker |
| A stack of five in different colours | Signals you can actually deliver a set |
